# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Parisian locals and visitors to Saint-Germain-des-Prés discovering or returning to Le Comptoir des Saints-Pères. They land on the site to check the menu, get the address, confirm hours, or share the restaurant with someone. Context: on mobile (metro, street), occasionally desktop at home. French-speaking primary audience; English-speaking tourists as secondary.

## Product Purpose

Marketing website for a heritage Parisian brasserie — the restaurant's digital presence. Success means a visitor feels the warmth and authenticity of the place before they arrive, finds the information they need in under 30 seconds, and leaves with intent to visit (or a reservation made).

## Brand Personality

Heritage · Parisian · Chaleureux (warm)

## Anti-references

- Generic food-delivery templates (dark background, neon accents, "Order Now" CTA dominance)
- Trendy tech-minimalist restaurants (stark white, Helvetica, brutalist spacing)
- TripAdvisor / Yelp aesthetic (star ratings, review cards, review-aggregator grey)
- Over-designed "fine dining" cold luxury (excessive whitespace, no warmth, intimidating)

## Design Principles

1. **Heritage before trend.** Every design decision should feel like it could belong to a 1920s Parisian brasserie menu card — refined, warm, typographically intentional.
2. **Warmth over perfection.** The restaurant is welcoming and unpretentious. The site should feel the same — inviting, not intimidating.
3. **Information is the product.** Visitors come for the menu, address, and hours. Never bury that behind decoration.
4. **French first.** Content, hierarchy, and copy voice lean French even in the English locale. The restaurant is proudly Parisian.
5. **Restraint on motion.** Animations should feel like a slow, confident reveal — never hurried or tech-startup energetic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline. Bilingual (FR/EN) — all visible content must be translatable. Reduced-motion support expected (scroll-triggered animations). No specific known user needs beyond standard AA compliance.
